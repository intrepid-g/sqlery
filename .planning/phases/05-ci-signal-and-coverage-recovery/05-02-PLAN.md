<objective>
Replace the emergency `13%` coverage floor with a defensible gate backed by a clean suite and a documented ratchet policy. The outcome should be honest and stricter than today, without pretending the codebase is already at some aspirational number it cannot sustain.
</objective>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Measure clean-suite coverage and choose the defended threshold</name>
  <files>(measurement only)</files>
  <read_first>
    - pyproject.toml
    - .github/workflows/test.yml
    - docs/TESTING.md
  </read_first>
  <action>
    Run the clean default suite with coverage after 05-01 lands and extract the TOTAL percentage from the terminal output.

    Use that measured number to choose a concrete threshold with this rule:
    - it must be materially above `13`
    - it must be low enough that the current clean suite can pass consistently
    - it must be high enough that regressions fail quickly

    Document the chosen threshold and the measured baseline in the phase summary. If the clean baseline is still too low to justify a useful gate, stop and surface that as a real planning problem rather than faking confidence.
  </action>
  <acceptance_criteria>
    - A real measured baseline is recorded from the clean suite.
    - The chosen threshold is explicitly justified from that baseline.
    - The executor does not jump straight to `70` unless the measured suite actually supports it.
  </acceptance_criteria>
</task>
