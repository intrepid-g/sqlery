### `t/unit/tasks/test_stamping.py`

Celery "stamping" = attaching labels/metadata to jobs (and to whole workflows), which then travel with the job and are stored with its result. Mapped to sqlery: job labels/metadata, dependencies (chain/group/chord), on-success / on-failure follow-up jobs, job replacement, retry.

**Labelling jobs and workflows**
- Label a single job; expect: the label is on the job and its label name is listed in the job's list of labelled names.
- Label values may be a bool, list, set, string or UUID; expect: each stored and readable back (list/set: every item present).
- Label every job of a workflow of any shape (single job, group, nested groups, chain, chord with group/generator/single-job header, chord whose body is a chain, 5 levels of nesting, mixed); expect: every real job carries the label and its name.
- Same workflow; expect: container wrappers (group/chain/chord) carry no label-name list themselves, only the real jobs do.
- Workflow whose jobs already have on-success and on-failure follow-ups; expect: follow-ups are labelled too, with their own follow-up-specific labels.
- Workflow already carrying old labels and follow-ups, cleaned, then relabelled with append off; expect: only the new labels remain.
- The labeller is handed the actual job being labelled (same identity, not a copy).

**Manual labels and merging**
- Apply label A, then label B, to one job, run it; expect: result stores both labels and both names.
- Same label name applied twice, second time with append on; expect: both values kept (a list); an unrelated list-valued label stays intact.
- Label with no labeller, append on, scalar then list; expect: values merged into one list.
- Labeller returns labels without declaring names, or declares them explicitly; expect: identical outcome (names inferred).
- Label a group of 2 jobs, run; expect: both jobs' results carry the label and name.
- Label a chord (2 header jobs + body), run; expect: header jobs' results carry the label and name.

**Follow-ups (success/failure callbacks)**
- Chain of job, group, chord, job with success and failure follow-ups attached, then labelled, run; expect: final result correct (20), every step's result labelled, follow-ups carry both the general and callback-specific labels.
- Attach follow-ups after labelling; expect: follow-ups are NOT labelled retroactively (no label names).
- Label, attach follow-ups, label again with a second labeller; expect: jobs carry both labellers' labels, follow-ups get only the second labeller's labels and names.

**Replacement**
- Job with labels replaces itself with a new workflow; expect: the replacement does not inherit the parent's labels or label names, and the replace hook fires.
- Replace hook labels the replacement; expect: follow-ups gain labels after replace but had none before; replacing ends the original job (ignored/terminated).
- Replace with a group (any construction form) or chain of 2 jobs after labelling; expect: both jobs carry the label and every declared label name exists as a value.
- Replace with chains that have their own success/failure follow-ups; expect: follow-ups' declared labels all present, jobs labelled consistently.

**Retry**
- Retried job rebuilt from its previous request; expect: label names and values preserved.

**Duplicates**
- Failing and succeeding jobs each with follow-ups, labelled in a group (with group failure follow-up), chord header (error callback on header allowed), chord body (not allowed), and chain; expect: no label is applied twice to a job or follow-up (labeller asserts it never sees an existing label).
- Nested dictionary label, append off; expect: existing value wins, not aggregated into a list.
- Nested dictionary label, append on; expect: shared key becomes [1, 2], unique key stays 1.
